➤ METHOD · HOW INTEGRA DETECTS

Five signals to detect any
intentional under-performance.

01
MARKETS

Order books move first.

Spot-fixing shows up on one player's prop first. In Case 095-X1, Player X1's Map 3 kill line dropped from 15.5 to 13.5 — a −2.0 point move ten minutes before the map.

→ DRIFT
Continuous polling · cross-referenced against that player's historic kill line at the same map.
02
PLAYERS

Performance Index

Every player has a statistical fingerprint — kill rate, damage, deaths, accuracy — measured against his own 90-day baseline. In Case 095-X1, Player X1 collapsed to a 0.61 rating on Ancient, his career-best map (1.19).

→ INDEX
KDR · KPR · ADR · DPR · Shot accuracy — all measured against the player's own baseline, never a tour average.
03
PATTERNS

What the eye cannot follow.

Reaction time, crosshair placement and decision latency are fingerprints a player can't fake under pressure. In Case 095-X1, first-contact reaction slowed from 125ms to 218.8ms and crosshair angle drifted +1.7° past his own range — on the exact map the line moved.

→ REFLEX · PHANTOM
Tick-precise reaction timing and crosshair tracking · frame-level demo parsing · all measured against the player's own 90-day window, never a tour average.
04
CHATTER

Someone always tells someone.

Before the map ends, someone has already said it. In Case 095-X1, public HLTV and Telegram posts named Player X1's under on Map 3 — pointing at a name, not a scoreline.

→ WATCH
Public HLTV, Telegram and Discord monitoring · phrases matched to the flagged player and surfaced before the map ends.
05
GAMEPLAY

Gameplay Analysis

Round-by-round review surfaces what statistics can't explain. In Case 095-X1, Player X1 took 10 disadvantaged peeks into multi-enemy holds on his career-best map.

→ GAMEPLAY
Round-by-round footage · disadvantaged peeks, unused utility and passive positioning matched against the player's own career pattern.
