MARKETS

Spot-fixing shows up on one player's prop first. In Case 095-X1, Player X1's Map 3 kill line dropped from 15.5 to 13.5 — a −2.0 point move ten minutes before the map.

→ DRIFT
Continuous polling · cross-referenced against that player's historic kill line at the same map.
02
PLAYERS

Performance Index

Every player has a statistical fingerprint — kill rate, damage, deaths, accuracy — measured against his own 90-day baseline. In Case 095-X1, Player X1 collapsed to a 0.61 rating on Ancient, his career-best map (1.19).

→ INDEX
KDR · KPR · ADR · DPR · Shot accuracy — all measured against the player's own baseline, never a tour average.

PATTERNS

What the eye cannot follow.

Reaction time, crosshair placement and decision latency are fingerprints a player can't fake under pressure. In Case 095-X1, first-contact reaction slowed from 125ms to 218.8ms and crosshair angle drifted +1.7° past his own range — on the exact map the line moved.

→ REFLEX · PHANTOM
Tick-precise reaction timing and crosshair tracking · frame-level demo parsing · all measured against the player's own 90-day window, never a tour average.
04
CHATTER

Someone always tells someone.

Before the map ends, someone has already said it. In Case 095-X1, public HLTV and Telegram posts named Player X1's under on Map 3 — pointing at a name, not a scoreline.

→ WATCH
Public HLTV, Telegram and Discord monitoring · phrases matched to the flagged player and surfaced before the map ends.

What is a Kill Line?

Bookmakers set an expected kill count for every player — bettors bet over or under it.

✦ Line increases → market expects over-performance
✦ Line drops → market expects under-performance

Spotting an approach

How fixers actually make first contact — friendly DMs, a "harmless" favour, a friend-of-a-friend with a tip. The pressure tactics, the grooming pattern, and the moment to walk away.

Your reporting duty

What every player and staff member is obliged to report, to whom, and how quickly. Confidential channels, the protections for those who come forward, and why silence is itself a breach.

Betting & code rules

What the rules actually prohibit — betting on your own game, passing inside information, courtsiding, manipulating a single round. The grey areas made black and white.

MODULE 04

What an investigation looks like

How integrity signals are reviewed, what evidence is and isn't, due process, and the presumption of innocence. Demystifying the process so players understand their rights as well as their duties.

MODULE 05

Protecting yourself

Practical habits: refusing and reporting an approach, keeping a record, managing your accounts and contacts, and avoiding the accidental breaches that end careers. Built for coaches to reinforce daily.
